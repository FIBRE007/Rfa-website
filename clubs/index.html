Before you register

Club selection rules

01

Choose one club. The portal should block duplicate active registrations for the same learner.

02

Section restrictions apply. Lower Primary clubs are for Grades 1–3; Upper Primary clubs are for Grades 4–5; Nursery clubs are for Kindergarten pupils.

03

Capacity is limited. Once a club reaches its maximum membership, it should no longer accept registrations.

04

Portal data remains authoritative. Final availability and learner details should come from the existing RFA portal.

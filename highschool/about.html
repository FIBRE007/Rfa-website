Welcome from the Director

A Christ-centred education that reaches beyond academics.

Royal Family Academy is committed to a nurturing, individualised and holistic learning experience in which academic growth, spiritual formation, character, creativity, leadership and service belong together.

At RFA, learners grow through rigorous academics, worship and discipleship, sports, the arts, STEM, mentoring and community service. The goal is not simply examination success, but the formation of young people who lead with compassion, integrity and resilience.

Continue reading the Director's message

Our educational programme brings together national and international standards, sound pedagogy and a biblical worldview. We are intentional about helping every learner discover gifts, develop confidence, think critically and use knowledge responsibly.

Leadership remains central to the RFA experience and is anchored on Excellence, Hard Work, Integrity, Innovation and Discipline. Through classroom learning and the wider life of the school, students are prepared to serve, solve problems and make a meaningful contribution wherever God places them.

We value partnership with families and invite prospective parents, current families, alumni and friends of the Academy to engage with the school community and visit the campus.

Miss Bunmi Ayeni
Director, Royal Family Academy

Our Story

High School since 2007.

Royal Family Academy began with its Nursery section in 2002 and expanded as the school community grew. The High School was inaugurated on 24 September 2007 at Wuye, Abuja.

From the beginning, the High School has combined national and international academic standards with a biblical worldview, leadership formation and a broad co-curricular experience designed to prepare students for tertiary education and responsible adulthood.

27 Sep 2002
RFA Nursery was commissioned by Pastors Ina and Sarah Omakwu.
30 Sep 2002
Academic activities began with 29 pupils and 17 members of staff.
2003
The Primary section commenced as the school expanded.
24 Sep 2007
Royal Family Academy High School was inaugurated at Wuye, Abuja.
20 Mar 2024
Royal Family Academy Sixth Form College was inaugurated.

Philosophy of Education

Education of spirit, mind and body.

RFA understands every learner as a gift entrusted by God. Education therefore develops the whole person through strong academics, biblical truth, character formation and responsible participation in society.

Christian foundation

Teaching and learning are approached from a Christian worldview, with Scripture shaping faith, character and the way knowledge is understood and applied.

Whole-person formation

Academic knowledge is not treated in isolation. RFA seeks growth in the learner's spirit, intellect, physical wellbeing, relationships, character and capacity to serve.

Stewardship of every learner

Learners are regarded as God's special gifts. The school accepts a responsibility to know, nurture, challenge and support them as they grow into responsible adults and positive influences.

Learning for life

The curriculum combines knowledge, skills, creativity, communication, critical thinking and collaboration with biblical values for further education, work, leadership and service.

Expected Student Outcomes

What an RFA education is working toward.

The RFA framework describes outcomes across four connected areas. Open any area to read the detailed expectations.

Intellectual Development
  • Develop strong knowledge across the sciences, arts and humanities.
  • Use information technology and languages effectively.
  • Communicate clearly in speaking and writing.
  • Think critically, collaborate and create.
  • Understand and apply biblical knowledge.
  • Identify and solve problems responsibly.
  • Prepare for tertiary education and lifelong learning.
Spiritual Development
  • Grow in a personal relationship with God.
  • Understand and explain the Christian faith thoughtfully.
  • Develop convictions and standards grounded in Scripture.
  • Recognise the work and ministry of the Holy Spirit.
  • Practise prayer, fasting, personal devotion, evangelism and Christian fellowship.
Physical Development
  • Practise personal hygiene and good grooming.
  • Understand the importance of rest, exercise, nutrition and wholesome leisure.
  • Respect and care for the body.
  • Develop habits that support healthy, responsible living.
Social Development
  • Grow in leadership, stewardship, service and responsibility.
  • Develop emotional intelligence and healthy relationships.
  • Show respect, empathy and cultural sensitivity.
  • Participate constructively in family, school, community and society.

Frequently Asked Questions

Useful things families ask about RFA.

When was the school established?

The High School was inaugurated on 24 September 2007. Royal Family Academy itself began with the Nursery section in September 2002.

What kind of school is RFA?

RFA is a Christian school and a subsidiary of Family Ministries International, with historic association to Family Worship Centre. Its programme integrates biblical worldview, discipleship, worship, service and character formation with academic learning.

Does RFA admit families of other faiths?

Learners from other faith backgrounds may be admitted while the school's Christian identity and programme remain clearly expressed.

What curriculum does RFA use?

RFA describes an integrated curriculum drawing from Nigerian and international approaches, including British and American elements, with biblical integration and global best-practice pedagogy.

What facilities support learning?

Facilities include air-conditioned classrooms, specialist laboratories, creative-arts spaces, the Discovery Centre, Montessori room, clinic, swimming pool, auditorium, cafeteria, library/e-Library and a stadium with track and field facilities.

Which languages are taught?

Language provision includes Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo, together with French, Spanish and Mandarin at different school levels.

How are learners supported?

Support includes the Discovery Centre/SEND provision, NILD Educational Therapy, individualised programmes, Academic Assistance Sessions and targeted enhancement.

How are parents involved?

Parent partnership includes Parent–Teacher Conferences, the Parent/Teacher Progressive Forum, Breakfast Meetings, the Parenting Institute and other school-home engagement opportunities.

What learner-to-teacher ratios has RFA published?

The High School planning ratio is approximately 15 learners to 1 teacher. Current class composition can vary, so families should confirm the present arrangement with the relevant school office.

Does RFA welcome national and international students?

Yes. RFA serves both Nigerian and international students.

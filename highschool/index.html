About High School

Educated for consequence.

Royal Family Academy's vision is to raise dynamic, distinguished and innovative Christian leaders to impact society, fulfilling 2 Timothy 3:17 — through a curriculum that integrates high academic standards with biblical values and global best-practice pedagogy.

Safety, Conduct & Parent Relations

A safe, respectful community.

  • Caring and respectful conduct is expected of every student.
  • Diligence in classwork, homework, projects and revision.
  • Attendance at academic and co-curricular programmes.
  • Attendance and punctuality are compulsory under the handbook's rules.
  • Zero tolerance for bullying, rude/unruly behaviour or abuse of any form.
  • Health and safety, fire drills, security, harassment/bullying, internet use and electronic-device rules are documented in the handbook.
  • Parent–Teacher Conferences and Parent/Teacher Progressive Forum are documented parent-engagement mechanisms.
  • Visitors must follow campus sign-in procedures.

Student Life

Clubs, societies and competition.

Model United Nations

Gives students opportunities to debate current international issues and represent RFA at junior UN-member level.

Duke of Edinburgh Award

Opportunities for new challenges, team play, intellectual curiosity, service and academic development. Students begin the Bronze cadre at 14 before progressing to Silver.

Community Service & Alumni

Leadership expressed through service.

RFA's former site records a long tradition of practical outreach to families and communities, alongside an organised alumni network.

Community impact

Service initiatives have included support for indigent families, orphanages, internally displaced persons, hospitals, autism centres, widows and other vulnerable groups. Projects have also included books and devotional materials, hygiene sensitisation, practical improvements and community-support activities.

RFA Alumni Association

The former site states that the RFA Alumni Association was formally established on 27 July 2019 and had grown to more than 250 Royal Ambassadors. Because office holders can change, current alumni leadership should be confirmed before relying on historical officer names.

Spiritual Formation

Faith, discipled weekly.

  • Chapel every Friday, across the school — compulsory.
  • Students Mentoring Sessions every Wednesday — compulsory for all High School students.
  • Whole-school fasting on the first Friday of resumption.
  • Senior High 3 Retreat.
  • Valedictory Service / Graduation Ceremonies.
  • Discipleship Training Sessions.

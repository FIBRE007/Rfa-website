Character & Philosophy

Leadership. Innovation. Discipline.

Leadership

An avenue through which God influences society.

Leadership is RFA's core value — a means through which God solves problems in society through godly principles. Students are developed to identify needs, rally resources, collaborate and communicate effectively.

Innovation

Project-based learning.

Project-based learning stimulates imaginative, efficient, timely and sustainable problem-solving, and develops alternative solutions to challenges.

Discipline

Strength of character, godly conduct.

Discipline is a vital factor shaping a person's direction. RFA develops strength of character and godly conduct through biblical principles and spiritual-formation exercises.

RFA Leadership Curriculum

Leadership is taught, practised and lived.

As a Christian leadership school, RFA equips learners through structured leadership courses and well-planned activities designed to develop integrity, critical thinking, service and readiness for school, career and life.

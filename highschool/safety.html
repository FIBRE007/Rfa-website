Safety, Conduct & Parent Relations

A safe, respectful community.

Student Conduct & Responsibility

Respect, diligence and responsibility.

  • Caring and respectful conduct is expected of every student.
  • RFA maintains zero tolerance for bullying, unruly behaviour or abuse.
  • Students are expected to work diligently in classwork, homework, projects and personal revision.
  • Attendance at academic, co-curricular and other school programmes is expected.
High School classroom responsibilityclassrooms/senior-high-lesson.jpg

Attendance & Punctuality

The school day begins with readiness.

  • Attendance is compulsory; valid absences should be communicated to the school.
  • Students are expected to be in school by 7:45 a.m.
  • Arrival after 7:55 a.m. activates the school's late-coming procedures before the student returns to class.
  • Assembly, Chapel and Class Devotion are compulsory parts of school life.

Campus Safety & Wellbeing

Clear rules protect learning.

  • Students are expected to leave spaces clean and care for school property.
  • Food and drinks are taken only in designated areas.
  • Mobile phones and electronic gadgets are not permitted except with a teacher's permission.
  • Students are expected to participate in emergency and safety drills.
  • Visitors follow the school's campus sign-in procedures.

Everyday School Life

Clear routines for learners and families.

Practical information for learners and families is presented here in a compact form.

Uniform & Appearance

Students wear the official RFA uniform supplied through the school. Uniform should be worn neatly and as prescribed, without unauthorised alterations. Grooming, hair and accessories are expected to reflect the school's standards of neatness, modesty and safety.

Visitors to Campus

Visitors are expected to sign in through the appropriate school office or secretary and follow campus procedures. Classroom access during lessons is restricted except for authorised school business such as a scheduled parent–teacher conference.

High School weekly rhythm
  • Monday Assembly: 7:45–8:30 a.m.
  • Tuesday to Friday Class Devotion: 7:45–8:15 a.m.
  • Wednesday Mentorship: 2:30–3:20 p.m., followed by Clubs from 3:20–4:00 p.m.
  • Thursday includes organised sport.
  • Friday includes Chapel and the closing rhythm of the school week.
School year

RFA operates a 40-week academic year running from September to July, organised into three terms of approximately thirteen weeks.

Homework & assignment policy

Homework reinforces learning, develops independent study habits and helps teachers and parents understand progress. Learners are expected to complete assigned work carefully and on time; teachers provide guidance and feedback, while parents support a suitable routine and environment for study. Late or incomplete work is handled through the school's academic and conduct procedures.

Non-discrimination

RFA's published admission policy states that students are admitted without discrimination on the basis of race, colour, nationality or ethnic origin. Employment is also described as non-discriminatory within the school's Christian character and faith-based requirements for staff.

Campus & Facilities

A place built for serious learning.

The High School Environment

Learning needs the right spaces.

High School students learn within the wider Royal Family Academy campus, with spaces that support academic depth, creativity, wellbeing, leadership and physical development.

From laboratories and ICT spaces to the auditorium and sporting facilities, the campus gives students room to learn, practise, collaborate and grow beyond the classroom.

Campus Facilities

Spaces that support the whole student.

  • Air-conditioned classrooms with student cubicles
  • Science laboratories
  • ICT laboratories
  • Home Economics / Home Management laboratories
  • Arts studios
  • School auditorium
  • Cafeteria
  • School clinic
  • Swimming pool
  • Stadium with track and field facilities

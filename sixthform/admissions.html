Sixth Form entry requirements

Applicants are expected to hold WASSCE, NECO, IGCSE or an equivalent qualification, with at least five relevant credits including English Language and Mathematics. An entrance assessment and student/parent interview form part of the admission process.

Programme requirements can change, so applicants should confirm the current requirement for their chosen pathway with the Sixth Form Admissions team.

Age appropriateness

Age appropriateness is considered alongside academic assessment and previous educational experience. The current age tables for each RFA school remain the first reference point.

Non-discrimination

Admission is open without discrimination on the basis of race, colour, nationality or ethnic origin.

University & Careers

Prepared for what's next.

Global Direction

From Sixth Form to university.

RFA Sixth Form is designed to prepare students for academic excellence and global opportunities, with pathways that can support progression to universities in the UK, USA, Canada, Nigeria and other destinations.

  • International University Foundation for university progression.
  • Cambridge A Level for internationally recognised academic preparation.
  • OSSD as a Canadian secondary-school qualification.
  • SAT preparation for university admissions.
  • IELTS preparation for international English-language requirements.
  • UTME tutorial support for Nigerian university admission.

Beyond Qualifications

Readiness means more than passing an examination.

The Sixth Form environment combines serious academic preparation with character development and growing independence, helping students make a more confident transition into tertiary education and leadership.

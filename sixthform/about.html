About Sixth Form

The bridge between secondary school and university.

Our Story

A deliberate next step.

Royal Family Academy Sixth Form College was inaugurated on Wednesday, 20 March 2024. It was established to give students a focused pre-university experience that broadens and deepens academic preparation before tertiary education.

The college is part of the wider Royal Family Academy journey and continues RFA's emphasis on academic excellence, character development and leadership while preparing students for local and international university pathways.

The Sixth Form Experience

Academic depth with global direction.

  • International University Foundation pathway.
  • Cambridge A Level.
  • Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD).
  • SAT and IELTS preparation.
  • UTME (JAMB) tutorial support.
  • Qualified and experienced teachers.
  • A supportive environment integrating academic excellence and character development.
  • Preparation for universities in the UK, USA, Canada and other destinations.

Why RFA Sixth Form

Preparation that looks beyond the next exam.

Students are prepared not only for examinations but for the transition into university life: stronger academic independence, clearer direction and the confidence to pursue global opportunities from within the secure Royal Family Academy campus in Abuja.

Examinations & International Connections

Pathways designed for university progression.

RFA Sixth Form's published materials connect its pre-university offer with international examination and university-entry pathways.

Programme pathways

International University Foundation, Cambridge A Level, Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD), SAT, IELTS and UTME/JAMB preparation support different university-entry routes.

Published examination relationships

The former site identified Cambridge International and the British Council among its academic/examination relationships and described RFA as an attached centre for Cambridge IGCSE/CIE.

Families should confirm the current examination-centre arrangement and programme availability directly with the Sixth Form College.

Programmes

Pathways built around what comes next.

RFA Sixth Form offers a range of internationally recognised qualifications and preparatory programmes designed to broaden academic perspective and prepare students for tertiary education in Nigeria and abroad.

Academic Pathways

Choose the route that fits the destination.

International University Foundation

A pre-university pathway designed to prepare students for progression into university study.

Cambridge A Level

A globally recognised qualification used for entry to universities in many countries.

Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD)

A Canadian secondary-school qualification recognised by universities internationally.

SAT

Preparation for the standardised admission test used by many universities in the United States and elsewhere.

IELTS

Preparation for the international English-language proficiency examination used for study abroad.

UTME (JAMB Tutorial)

Focused preparation for Nigerian university admission through the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination.

The RFA Advantage

Serious preparation. Personal support.

  • Qualified and experienced teachers.
  • Support for local and international examinations.
  • Academic preparation alongside character development.
  • Pathways toward universities in the UK, USA, Canada and beyond.
  • A secure learning environment within the RFA campus.

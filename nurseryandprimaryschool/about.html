About

Raising Distinguished Leaders — from the start.

Royal Family Academy's vision is to raise dynamic, distinguished and innovative Christian leaders to impact society, fulfilling 2 Timothy 3:17. Our mission is to take each child through an educational programme using a systematic application of the word of God and an integrated educational curriculum to impact society.

As a Christian school, RFA interweaves academic excellence and spiritual formation, developing the spirit, mind and body of young people in preparation for adult life in a dynamic world. RFA describes students as God's special gifts to humanity, and the school as a steward called to nurture them into instruments of reconciliation and healing.

Our Aims

What we are building toward.

  • Develop learners who are knowledgeable and proficient in all areas of learning.
  • Develop learners with a biblical worldview consistent with the school's faith.
  • Develop intellectual, social and leadership skills.
  • Develop a heart of service and stewardship.
  • Build a motivated and skilled faculty with appropriate qualifications/certification and professional development.
  • Run a curriculum integrating high standards of knowledge, skills and biblical values, using pedagogy that meets global best practices.
  • Provide adequate, safe, well-maintained and aesthetically welcoming facilities.
  • Encourage parents to participate actively in their children's learning and school programmes.
